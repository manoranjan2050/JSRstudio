About JSR Studio

We Capture Stories, Not Just Photographs.

JSR Studio is dedicated to capturing genuine emotions, beautiful moments and unforgettable memories through professional photography, proudly based in Jagatsinghpur, Odisha.

We provide photography services for weddings, events, portraits, birthdays, pre-wedding sessions and other special occasions — bringing a thoughtful, creative eye to every frame.
